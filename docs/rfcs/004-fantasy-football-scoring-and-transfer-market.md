## 1. Abstract & Scope

Fantasy Football provides continuous retention and user engagement alongside traditional fixed-odds sportsbook wagering. In the Virtual Football Universe (VFL), where matches simulate in compressed cycles (480 seconds per matchday), the scoring engine, transfer market valuation curves, and automatic bench substitution logic must operate deterministically, synchronously, and at high velocity.

RFC 004 establishes:
1. The authoritative points scoring matrix adapted from FPL for virtual simulations.
2. Dynamic player valuation and transfer pricing formulas based on form and net ownership deltas.
3. Squad constraints: 15-player squad, budget caps (100.0M), and club quotas.
4. Auto-substitution and captaincy multiplier rules.

## 3. Dynamic Transfer Market & Player Valuation

Player values fluctuate dynamically between gameweeks to emulate a live stock market.

### 3.1 Valuation Formula
Let $V_t$ be a player's valuation at gameweek $t$.

$$V_{t+1} = \text{clamp}\left(V_t + \Delta V_{form} + \Delta V_{demand}, V_{min}, V_{max}\right)$$

where:
1. **Performance Drift**:
   $$\Delta V_{form} = \begin{cases} 
         +0.2\text{M} & \text{if } \text{Points}_{GW} \ge 10 \\
         +0.1\text{M} & \text{if } 7 \le \text{Points}_{GW} < 10 \\
         0.0\text{M} & \text{if } 3 \le \text{Points}_{GW} < 7 \\
         -0.1\text{M} & \text{if } \text{Points}_{GW} < 3 
      \end{cases}$$

2. **Net Ownership Elasticity**:
   $$\Delta V_{demand} = \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{TransfersIn} - \text{TransfersOut}}{K_{liquidity}} \times 0.1\text{M}, 1 \right)$$
   with $K_{liquidity} = 500\text{ traders}$.

3. **Bounding Limits**:
   $$V_{min} = 4.0\text{M} \quad\text{and}\quad V_{max} = 15.5\text{M}$$

## 4. Squad Composition Constraints

Every trader's squad must rigorously satisfy:
- **Total Roster Size**: Exactly 15 players.
- **Positional Quotas**:
  - $2 \times \text{Goalkeepers}$
  - $5 \times \text{Defenders}$
  - $5 \times \text{Midfielders}$
  - $3 \times \text{Forwards}$
- **Starting XI Formation**: Must include 1 Goalkeeper and at least 3 Defenders, 2 Midfielders, and 1 Forward (valid shapes: 3-5-2, 3-4-3, 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-5-1, 5-3-2, 5-4-1, 5-2-3).
- **Club Limit**: A maximum of 3 players from any single club.
- **Budget Ceiling**: Initial squad total purchase price $\le 100.0\text{M}$ minor currency equivalent.

---

## 5. Captaincy Multipliers & Auto-Substitutions

1. **Captain (C)**: Player's gameweek score is multiplied by $2\times$.
2. **Vice-Captain (VC)**: If the selected Captain records 0 minutes, the $2\times$ multiplier automatically transfers to the Vice-Captain.
3. **Automated Bench Substitutions**:
   - If an outfield starter plays 0 minutes, the engine checks bench players in priority order (Bench 1, 2, 3).
   - A bench substitute is promoted if and only if the resulting formation satisfies minimum formation criteria (at least 3 DEF, 2 MID, 1 FWD).
   - Goalkeeper substitute only replaces the starting Goalkeeper.
